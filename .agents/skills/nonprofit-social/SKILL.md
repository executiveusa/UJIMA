# Nonprofit Social

## Governing skill

Inherit `.agents/skills/nonprofit-operating-system/SKILL.md`.

## Mission

Run social as a trust, discovery, participation, and proof system—not as a posting quota.

## Content purpose

Every planned item must serve at least one purpose:
- IDENTITY
- TRUST
- PROOF
- PARTICIPATION
- DISCOVERY
- PARTNERSHIP
- DONATION
- RECRUITMENT

If it serves none, remove it.

## Source priority

Use real founder language, real participant/community moments, real photographs, real documentary footage, and verified outcomes. Search existing interviews/transcripts before requesting new copy.

Never invent quotes, impact claims, program names, or statistics.

## Default rhythm

When appropriate:
- Monday: identity / belief / mission
- Wednesday: founder / documentary / Reel
- Friday: community / participation / action

Adapt cadence to the client. Do not post just to remain active.

## Channel fit

Use Instagram/Facebook as the default community layer; YouTube for durable video; TikTok when audience/content fit exists; LinkedIn when partnership-facing; Google Business Profile for local discovery.

## Workflow

SYSTEM DETECTS opportunity → SYSTEM PREPARES copy/assets/schedule → HUMAN CONFIRMS consequential public material.

Safe automation: drafts, resizing, scheduling, tagging, reporting, reminders.
Approval required for sensitive statements, major fundraising claims, controversial messaging, major brand changes, legal claims, consequential partnerships, or irreversible publication decisions.

## Verification

Do not call content published until the target channel is checked. Track what was published, reach/views, meaningful actions, top performer, and learning for the next cycle.

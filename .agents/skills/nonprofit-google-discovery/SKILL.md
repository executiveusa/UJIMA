# Nonprofit Google / Discovery

## Governing skill

Inherit `.agents/skills/nonprofit-operating-system/SKILL.md`.

## Mission

Increase qualified discovery without forcing unnecessary tools on the client.

## Always investigate

- Google for Nonprofits eligibility
- Google Ad Grants eligibility
- YouTube nonprofit features
- Google Business Profile
- Search Console
- Analytics
- local search visibility

Do not force Google Workspace or Gmail when the organization already has an email system it prefers.

## Discovery model

Treat discovery as the front of:

**DISCOVERY → TRUST → ACTION → FOLLOW-UP → RELATIONSHIP**

Search visibility is only useful when the destination page is credible and the next action works.

## Execution checks

For applicable clients:
- verify organization name, address/service area, contact details, hours, categories, and website URL;
- verify Search Console ownership and sitemap/indexing state;
- verify Analytics installation and meaningful conversion events;
- identify branded and local queries;
- identify obvious metadata/indexability problems;
- document eligibility gaps for nonprofit products rather than guessing.

## Proof standard

Never claim eligibility, approval, indexing, ranking, or ad-grant activation without evidence.

## Client output

Translate platform data into outcomes, for example:
“312 people found the organization this month; 27 took a meaningful action.”

Do not hand the client a dashboard as the primary deliverable.

# Nonprofit Video

## Governing skill

Inherit `.agents/skills/nonprofit-operating-system/SKILL.md`. Reuse `browser-video-editor` and `synthcut-local-video` where useful.

## Mission

Create human evidence, not generic “content.” Video should make the organization more credible, understandable, and useful.

## Source priority

Prefer:
- exact founder language;
- participant/community voice with appropriate consent;
- documentary footage;
- natural sound;
- real environments;
- authentic interactions;
- restrained titles;
- intentional pacing.

Avoid:
- AI voice when a real voice exists;
- stock footage;
- fake cinematic drama;
- generic inspirational music;
- excessive captions;
- overproduced nonprofit montages.

## Repeatable formats

1. Founder belief clip
2. Why we started
3. One person / one story
4. Community proof
5. Volunteer invitation
6. Partner invitation
7. What happens next
8. Event recap
9. Youth/community spotlight
10. Donation impact story

## Production rule

Start from the strongest authentic sentence or moment, then build the smallest edit that preserves its truth. Do not add effects that compete with the subject.

## Approval boundaries

Require approval for sensitive beneficiary stories, youth-identifying material, consequential public claims, fundraising claims, or any edit that materially changes meaning.

## Verification

Before release verify:
- source rights/consent where required;
- exact quotes against source;
- aspect ratio and platform-safe framing;
- captions/titles for accuracy;
- audio intelligibility;
- export quality;
- final published location when publishing is in scope.

Report the result as outcome and evidence, not editing-tool activity.

## Grid-preflight law

Before any Feed post is scheduled or published, the agent must:

1. Read the current ASC3ND Instagram profile.
2. Detect pinned posts, manual posts, archived posts, deletions, and visible ordering changes.
3. Inspect at least the latest twelve Feed items.
4. Compare the current profile with the approved grid manifest.
5. Simulate where the proposed post will appear.
6. Render or record a post-publication grid preview.
7. Stop if the proposed post breaks the intended row or visual rhythm.
8. Obtain human approval.
9. Send the approved item to Postiz as a draft or approved scheduled record.
10. Verify the live profile after publishing.

## Fail-closed conditions

The agent must not publish when:

- the current profile cannot be read;
- the proposed asset has no registered grid position;
- a manual post, pin, deletion, archive, or layout change shifts the expected sequence;
- the current profile differs from the grid manifest;
- required media, transcript, consent, subtitle, or editorial approvals are missing;
- human approval is missing.

## Event-day rule

Event-day coverage uses Stories and Reels. It does not add an unplanned standalone Feed tile that would disrupt the four-row profile system.

## Postiz law

1. Import records as drafts only.
2. `publish` must remain `false` until a human approval record exists.
3. `grid_preflight_required` must remain `true` for every primary Feed post.
4. Do not alter asset IDs or relative paths.
5. Do not publish transcript-based video captions until transcript, footage, consent, speaker labels, subtitles, and editorial selection are approved.
6. Do not identify minors or expose private youth/family information.
7. Do not insert sponsor names, event results, partner claims, or impact numbers until verified.
8. Do not regenerate, distort, decorate, or stylize the locked QR.

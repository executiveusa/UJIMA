# UJIMA — Heart & Soul

## Heart

UJIMA exists to return time, attention and agency to people doing work that matters.

Technology should carry administrative burden so people can carry judgment, relationships, imagination and purpose.

UJIMA should not make an organization better at operating software. It should make the organization better at fulfilling its mission.

## Soul

UJIMA works quietly.

It does not pretend.  
It does not fabricate evidence.  
It does not exploit trauma for engagement.  
It does not force organizations to become technology companies.  
It remembers what people already told it.  
It prepares before asking.  
It asks when judgment matters.  
It explains consequential actions.  
It leaves evidence.  
It makes mistakes recoverable.  
It respects language, culture, dignity and local context.  
It belongs to the organization running it.

## Governing laws

PEOPLE BEFORE SOFTWARE.

OUTCOMES BEFORE FEATURES.

TRUTH BEFORE CONFIDENCE.

PREPARE BEFORE ASKING.

AUTOMATE BURDEN, NOT JUDGMENT.

ONE FACT, ONE HOME.

ONE HUMAN-FACING SYSTEM.

PROOF BEFORE CLAIMS.

SHIP ONLY WHAT CAN COME BACK.

MORE POWER. LESS INTERFACE.

THE ORGANIZATION OWNS THE SYSTEM.

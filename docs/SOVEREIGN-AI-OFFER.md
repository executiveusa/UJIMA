## What Mission OS is

Mission OS is a privately deployed AI operating system built for nonprofits, social-purpose organizations, and mission-driven teams in the Pacific Northwest.

It runs on a server you control. Your data stays in your database. Your AI agents follow your rules — and cannot take sensitive actions without human approval.

You are not renting software. You are receiving a configured, working system that you own outright.

## What your AI agents can do

With Mission OS, your agents can help your team:

- Draft grant proposals, outreach emails, and program summaries — for human review before sending
- Summarize programs, documents, and reports
- Generate social media drafts — for staff approval before posting
- Answer internal questions about your programs, policies, and documents
- Assist with volunteer coordination, donor outreach, and event planning — all with human review
- Support staff during busy seasons without requiring new hires

Every AI output goes through your team before it reaches anyone outside your organization.

---

## What agents cannot do without your approval

Your system is configured with hard blocks on sensitive actions. Without explicit human approval, agents cannot:

- Submit grant applications or funding requests
- Send any message to a donor, funder, or community member
- Post anything to social media
- Make any legal, financial, or compliance filing
- Access another organization's data

These are not optional restrictions. They are built into the system.

## How implementation works

Implementation takes approximately 4–6 weeks depending on your organization's complexity and staff availability.

1. **Discovery** (Week 1) — We review your workflows, programs, and staff roles
2. **Configuration** (Week 2) — We set up your tenant configuration, agent pack, and knowledge base
3. **Deployment** (Week 3) — We provision your server and deploy all services
4. **Training** (Week 4) — Staff onboarding and operator training
5. **Handoff** (Week 5–6) — You receive all access, credentials, and documentation

You are live with a working system by week 6.

## This is not a SaaS subscription

Mission OS is a one-time setup engagement. You pay for the implementation. You own the result. There is no forced monthly subscription to Asc3nd.

You do pay directly for:
- Your VPS hosting (typically $20–$80/month depending on size)
- Your AI model API usage (billed directly to your account with the model provider)
- Any third-party tools you choose to connect

These costs go directly to those providers — not through Asc3nd.

## Who this is not right for

Mission OS is not the right fit if your organization:

- Needs fully autonomous AI with no human review
- Wants a turnkey product with 24/7 vendor support included
- Requires legal compliance certification (HIPAA, FERPA, COPPA) — we do not provide compliance certification
- Does not have any staff member available to learn basic system administration

## Objective

Use the existing approved Instagram grid architecture to give ASC3ND a complete, understandable posting plan from the start of the campaign through the event. Mix static brand/event posts with founder/interview video so the organization explains who it is, what it does, why the event matters, and how families can participate.

This sprint is also the first three weeks of the contracted 12-week content calendar. It does not by itself satisfy the full 12-week calendar or the full 30-caption bank.

## Video workflow

Each Wednesday Reel must pass:

`source footage -> transcript selection -> factual check -> edit script -> rough cut -> subtitles -> brand end card -> consent/privacy check -> internal review -> client approval -> scheduling draft -> live verification`

The Video Agent may propose selects and scripts. It may not invent quotes, event outcomes, sponsor claims, impact numbers, or statements not supported by the source footage or approved strategy.

## Video deliverable targets

- 9:16 vertical master
- 20–35 seconds preferred for the three pre-event Reels
- immediate hook in first 1–2 seconds
- burned-in or platform-safe subtitles
- clean speaker labeling only when verified
- ASC3ND-approved visual identity
- IG-30 vertical end card where appropriate
- no generated replacement of actual founder testimony

## Approval gates

Before a Feed post is scheduled:

1. Inspect the live Instagram grid.
2. Detect manual posts, pins, deletions, archives, or order changes.
3. Simulate the proposed grid position.
4. Confirm correct asset ID and caption.
5. Confirm video/transcript/consent approval when applicable.
6. Obtain human approval.
7. Schedule as draft/approved record.
8. Verify after publishing.

- the first three weeks are registered inside the full 12-week contract calendar rather than existing as a disconnected campaign.

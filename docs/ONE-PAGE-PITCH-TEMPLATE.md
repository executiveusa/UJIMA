## What Mission OS does

Mission OS gives your team an AI-assisted operations system that:

- Drafts grant proposals, outreach emails, and program summaries — **for your review before sending**
- Answers internal questions about your programs, policies, and documents
- Logs every AI action in an audit trail your staff can see
- Blocks sensitive actions (submitting grants, sending messages, posting to social media) **until a human approves**

Everything AI-generated is reviewed by your team. Nothing goes out automatically.
